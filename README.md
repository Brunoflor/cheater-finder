# 🕵️ Cheater Finder

**Customer database analysis for small and medium betting agencies: find the cheaters before they cost you.**

Big operators have whole risk teams. Small and medium agencies usually don't. Cheater Finder lets them analyze their own customer database and spot the bettors who are likely exploiting them.

## Three bettor profiles
Every customer is classified into one of three profiles:

| Profile | Behavior | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ **Profitable** | Wins consistently on top leagues | Follow |
| 🍀 **Lucky** | Wins in the short term, loses in the long run | Monitor |
| 🚩 **Cheater** | Bets on low leagues (e.g. Ukrainian third tier, Brazilian Serie B) where the risk of match-fixing is high | Avoid |

## Who it's for
Small and medium betting agencies that want to protect their margins without building an in-house risk team.
